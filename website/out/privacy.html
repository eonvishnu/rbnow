1. Information We Collect

In compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable data protection regulations, The Royal Bengal Fresh collects personal information when you browse our platform, create an account, or place an order:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full name, verified mobile number, and email address.
  • Delivery & Location Data: Exact GPS location coordinates (latitude & longitude), street addresses, house numbers, and delivery pincodes.
  • Transaction Records: Order history, item preferences, and invoice records.
  • Device & Usage Metadata: Browser type, IP address, session tokens, and security audit logs.

2. Use of Information & Data Security

Your personal data is utilized strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Order Fulfillment: Processing, cleaning, temperature-packing, and dispatching fresh perishable goods to your exact GPS location.
  • Customer Communication: Sending order confirmations, dispatch alerts, OTP verification, and WhatsApp/SMS delivery updates.
  • Third-Party Logistics Sharing: Sharing necessary delivery details (recipient name, phone number, and delivery address/coordinates) with trusted logistics partners (such as Rapido, Porter, Uber, Dunzo) strictly to deliver your order. We never sell customer data.
  • Platform Security: Preventing fraudulent transactions and securing administrative sessions.

We implement industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/SSL) and secure database encryption and strict access controls to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
